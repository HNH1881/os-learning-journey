# 10. Weekly Review / 每周复盘

## Weekly structure / 周复盘结构

English:
- what I learned
- what I practiced
- mistakes and confusion
- open questions
- next week plan

中文:
- 本周学到什么
- 做了什么实践
- 遇到什么困惑
- 未解决问题
- 下周计划

## Reflection rule / 复盘原则

English:
A weekly review should turn passive reading into active understanding and identify weak links in your knowledge.

中文:
每周复盘应该把被动阅读转化为主动理解，并找出知识中的薄弱环节。
